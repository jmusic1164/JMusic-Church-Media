How to Build a Simple Church Livestream Setup

A practical beginner guide to building a reliable church livestream without making the setup unnecessarily complicated.

ADVERTISEMENT

1. Start with the basic chain

A simple setup can be understood as: Camera → Computer → Streaming software → Internet → Streaming platform. Your audio should be treated as a separate priority: Microphone/Mixer → Computer or camera → Stream.

2. Prioritize audio

Viewers will often tolerate imperfect video, but poor or inconsistent audio makes a livestream difficult to follow. Test microphone levels before the service and avoid clipping.

3. Use OBS or your preferred software

Create scenes for the opening, worship, sermon, announcements and closing. Keep text large enough to read on a phone and avoid overcrowding the screen.

4. Test before going live

  • Camera framing and focus
  • Microphone level
  • Music and speech balance
  • Internet upload stability
  • Power and cable connections
  • Correct livestream destination

5. Keep a backup plan

Prepare backup cables, a backup presentation file and a simple fallback scene. Write the most important steps down so another volunteer can help when needed.

Media team tip: Create a one-page pre-service checklist and use the same workflow every week. Consistency reduces mistakes.
