How to Use a Webcam for Church Service

A simple workflow for using a USB webcam with a computer for church presentation or livestreaming.

Connect the webcam

Connect the webcam directly to the computer when possible. Give the camera a stable position and check that the lens is clean.

Select it in your software

In your presentation, recording or streaming software, choose the webcam as the video source. Check resolution and framing before service.

Improve the picture

  • Place the camera near eye level.
  • Face the subject toward the light.
  • Avoid a bright window directly behind the speaker.
  • Keep the background clean.
Tip: Good lighting can improve a budget webcam more than simply buying a higher-resolution camera.
