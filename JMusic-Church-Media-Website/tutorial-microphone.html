Microphone Basics for Church Media Teams

Understand the basics before buying or connecting microphones for worship, preaching and livestreaming.

Choose for the job

Handheld, lavalier, headset and USB microphones serve different purposes. Start with the use case rather than the brand name.

Watch your levels

Set a healthy input level and leave headroom. If the signal clips, lowering the level later may not restore the lost quality.

Reduce unwanted noise

  • Keep cables and connectors in good condition.
  • Separate audio cables from strong power sources where practical.
  • Position microphones consistently.
  • Listen with headphones during testing.
Media team tip: Make a short soundcheck routine and use it every service.
