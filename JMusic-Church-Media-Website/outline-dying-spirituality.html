Are You Slowly Dying Spiritually?

A simple self-examination outline for Christian teaching, Bible study or devotional use.

“Therefore to him that knoweth to do good, and doeth it not, to him it is sin.” — James 4:17

10 possible warning signs

  1. Losing your passion — spiritual things no longer receive the attention they once did.
  2. Wrong priorities — temporary things consistently replace spiritual priorities.
  3. Isolation — withdrawing from healthy Christian fellowship and encouragement.
  4. Complaining attitude — focusing more on problems than opportunities to serve.
  5. Making excuses — repeatedly finding reasons not to do what you know is right.
  6. Oversensitivity — allowing correction or small disappointments to push you away.
  7. Finding the negative — noticing faults while overlooking what is good.
  8. Reading without learning — reading Scripture without allowing it to shape your life.
  9. Listening without hearing — receiving teaching without putting it into practice.
  10. Attacking spiritual authority — responding to biblical correction with hostility rather than reflection.

Reflection

Use the list as a personal reflection tool, not simply as a way to judge others. Ask: “What needs to change in my own life?”

Suggested references: James 4:17; Hebrews 10:24–25; 1 Peter 4:10; 1 John 2:15–17; Matthew 5:14–16; Matthew 6:33; Romans 12:9–21; Matthew 7:1–5.
